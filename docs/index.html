Three steps, zero friction

No account, no extension, no desktop install. Works on phone and laptop alike.

  1. 01

    Copy the link

    Use the native Share → Copy link option in the app, or grab the URL from your browser bar.

  2. 02

    Paste into Saveflow

    Drop it in the box above and hit Download. We resolve the post and read its real media sources.

  3. 03

    Pick your quality

    Choose a resolution — or audio only — and the file saves straight to your device.

Built to be fast and quiet

Saveflow does one job and gets out of the way.

Direct source files

Links point at the platform's own CDN, so downloads run at full speed and skip re-encoding.

Nothing stored

We never keep your links or the media. Every request is resolved in memory and then forgotten.

Every screen size

One responsive layout that stays usable from a 360px phone up to an ultrawide monitor.

Audio extraction

When a post carries a separate audio track, Saveflow offers it as its own download.
